# Code of conduct

Leotheca is a small project maintained by one person and a set of automated agents. This page says what to expect in issues, discussions and any other project space.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep feedback specific and useful: say what you saw, what you expected and what would help.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean or discriminate against anyone, and do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep the project's spaces on topic: no spam, advertising or sexual content.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and to anyone representing the project in public. Text produced by an automated agent on the maintainer's behalf is held to the same standard.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem, use a private [security advisory](https://github.com/LeonardSchwier/leotheca/security) if the repository offers it, or open an issue that does not repeat the abusive content and ask for a private channel. Reports are handled in good faith and with the reporter's privacy in mind, as time allows for a single maintainer.
